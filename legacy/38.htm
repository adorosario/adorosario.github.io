Janet Pacheco

  • Born: 29 Jul 1956, Margao, Goa, India
  • Married: Jun 1986, Margao, Goa, India

Janet married Erlick Do Rosario, son of Caetano Do Rosario and Lindarifa Fernandes, in Jun 1986 in Margao, Goa, India. (Erlick Do Rosario was born on 11 Dec 1962 in Margao, Goa, India.)

   Marriage Notes:

Notes: 08/23/02
Erlick and Janet were married at Holy Spirit Church and the reception was then held at Clube Harmonia, Margao. This was the only wedding witnessed by Erlick's dad Caetano before he died. After his wedding, Erlick left for America to join Janet (who was already settled there)
